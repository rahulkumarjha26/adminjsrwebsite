  • Legally Binding Agreements: Standard NDAs for all clients and custom NDAs tailored to your specific requirements. Clear confidentiality clauses covering all project materials.
  • Strict Access Controls: Only authorized team members with security clearance access your content. Role-based access restrictions and clear "need-to-know" protocols.

Enterprise-Grade Security. No Exceptions.

Your Data Protection is Non-Negotiable

In an era where data breaches make headlines, security isn't a luxury—it's mandatory. Admin JSR implements enterprise-grade security protocols across all our operations to ensure your data remains protected, accessible only to authorized personnel, and compliant with global standards.

Our Security Infrastructure

  • Secure File Transfer & Storage: Encrypted file transfer protocols (SFTP, secure cloud storage) and AES-256 encryption for data at rest, alongside end-to-end encryption for sensitive transmissions.
  • Access Control & Authentication: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all systems, role-based access controls (RBAC), unique user credentials, and detailed audit trails.
  • Network Security: Firewalls and intrusion detection systems, VPN-protected internal networks, isolated project environments, and regular security updates.
  • Data Handling Protocols: Secure data deletion procedures, regular backup and disaster recovery plans, and a documented chain of custody.

Compliance & Certifications

We maintain active compliance with ISO 27001 (Information Security Management), ISO 9001 (Quality Management), and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

What This Means for You

Your data with Admin JSR is encrypted during transfer and storage, accessible only to authorized and trained personnel, protected by multi-layered security systems, and monitored 24/7 for threats.

Zero Tolerance for Mediocrity.

Every Deliverable is Verified, Validated, and Verified Again.

Quality isn't a destination—it's a process. At Admin JSR, every project goes through rigorous, multi-stage quality assurance protocols. By the time your deliverable reaches you, it has been checked, cross-checked, and validated by experienced linguists and QA specialists.

Our Multi-Layer QA Process

  • Layer 1: Native Linguist Review: Completed by a native speaker who reviews their own work for accuracy, tone, context, and terminology.
  • Layer 2: Peer Review & Editing: A second independent linguist reviews the work to check formatting, consistency, and style alignment.
  • Layer 3: QA Specialist Verification: A dedicated QA team checks compliance with file encoding, technical specifications, and client glossaries.
  • Layer 4: Final Sign-Off: The Project Manager conducts a final review to verify that all client requirements and metadata are complete.

Linguistic & Technical Checks

We verify native-speaker fluency, proper grammar and spelling, cultural appropriateness, timestamp accuracy, format compliance, and brand voice consistency.
